# Wedding-day invitation video — Google Flow prompts

**Shubham weds Preeti · Shubh Vivah · Thursday, 3 December 2026**

- **Length:** about 75 seconds, built from 10 clips of 8 seconds each.
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical for WhatsApp and Instagram. Switch to 16:9 for YouTube or TV.
- **Text:** Veo can't write text reliably, especially Hindi. Every prompt says "no text", and you add the on-screen text yourself in an editor such as CapCut, Canva or InShot.
- **Faces:** use Flow's **Ingredients to Video** and upload `public/photos/groom.jpg` and `public/photos/bride.jpg` as references for every clip that shows the couple, so they look the same in each clip.

## 5. Assembly tips

- **Music:** mute the audio Veo generates in clips 6 to 10 and lay one continuous shehnai track over the whole video, so it doesn't cut every 8 seconds. Keep Veo's dhol and chanting audio in clips 3 and 8 if you like it.
- **Pace:** use 1-second cross-dissolves between clips, and slow clips 6, 7 and 9 slightly (90%) for a dreamier feel.
- **Text:** use one serif font for English (Cormorant or Playfair) and one Devanagari font (Tiro Devanagari or Rozha One), in cream or gold, centred, with a soft shadow. That matches the website.
- **Reruns:** if a clip comes out wrong, regenerate it 2–3 times. Faces and garlands vary between runs.
- **Hotel exterior:** clip 5 is a generic heritage hotel, not the real Hotel Regency. If you want the real building, use a real photo of it instead.
